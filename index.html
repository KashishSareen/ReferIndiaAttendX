Our Process

From install to insights in 6 steps

1

Device Installation

Install one device at the entry gate and the second at the exit gate.

2

Card Registration

Register and assign RFID/NFC cards to students, employees or staff.

3

Tap & Record

Users simply tap their cards when entering or leaving the premises.

4

Auto Tracking

The system automatically records the matching entry or exit activity.

5

Telegram Alerts

Receive real-time notifications on your dedicated Telegram channel.

6

Access Reports

View attendance records and daily activity in the web application.
